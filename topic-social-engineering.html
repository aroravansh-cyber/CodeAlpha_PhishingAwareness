Topic 04

Social Engineering

The five levers

  • Authority — "This is IT support" or "This is your manager."
  • Urgency — "Act within the hour or your account is locked."
  • Fear — "Unauthorized charge detected on your card."
  • Curiosity — "See who viewed your profile."
  • Trust — a familiar name, logo, or writing style.

Why these work

Each lever is aimed at getting you to react before you think. None of them require a technical flaw — they work on attention and habit.

What to notice

Ask what emotion the message is trying to produce in you. Noticing the emotion is often the fastest way to notice the manipulation.
