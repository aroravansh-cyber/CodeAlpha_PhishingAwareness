Topic 05

Common Red Flags

Signs worth pausing on

  • A sender address that doesn't match who it claims to be
  • A domain that looks almost right
  • A request you weren't expecting
  • Pressure to act immediately
  • Threatening or alarming language
  • A request for a password or code
  • A request involving money or gift cards
  • An attachment you didn't ask for
  • A link that doesn't match its visible text
What to notice

One red flag alone isn't proof. Two or three together are worth stopping for.
