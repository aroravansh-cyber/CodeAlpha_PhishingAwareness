Topic 02

How Phishing Works

The general flow

Message arrives ↓ Trust or urgency is created ↓ You're asked to act ↓ Potential compromise

Each stage is designed to move you along before you stop to check anything.

Where the flow breaks

You can interrupt this chain at any point simply by pausing. Verifying independently — through a known number or bookmark, not a link in the message — breaks the flow before the last step.

A note on this course

This page explains the pattern so you can recognize it. It doesn't provide operational steps for running a real phishing attempt.
