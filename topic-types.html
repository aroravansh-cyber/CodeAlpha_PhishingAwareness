Topic 03

Types of Phishing

Six common forms

  • Email phishing — the classic form, sent to many people at once.
  • Smishing — phishing delivered by text message.
  • Vishing — a phone call pretending to be your bank, IT team, or a government office.
  • Spear phishing — a message tailored to one specific person or role.
  • QR-code phishing — a scanned code that leads to a fake page instead of a link.
  • Impersonation — posing as a coworker, executive, or known contact.
What to notice

The channel changes, but the pattern from the previous page — trust, then urgency, then action — stays the same.
