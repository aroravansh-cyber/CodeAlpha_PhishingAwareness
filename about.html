About

Why LearnPhish exists

What this is

LearnPhish is a small, self-contained education project about phishing. It doesn't send real emails, collect real credentials, or run real campaigns — it exists to teach the pattern behind phishing so it's easier to recognize in the wild.

Why it matters

Phishing doesn't succeed by breaking software; it succeeds by borrowing trust and rushing a decision. Most people can catch it once they know what to look for — the goal here is building that instinct.

How the journey works

The site follows four steps: Learn the concepts across eight short topics, Practice on fictional examples, take the Test to check your instincts, and see your Result.

What Practice does

Practice shows six fictional email scenarios. You mark each as suspicious or likely safe, then see the reasoning behind the correct answer — no real data is used or collected.

What the Phish Test does

The test is 20 scenario-based questions covering senders, links, urgency, and social engineering. Twelve or more correct is a pass; your latest result is saved locally in your browser.

What ClueWell-Mail is

ClueWell-Mail is a separate, optional demo that looks for educational signals — like urgency or credential requests — in email text you paste in. It's a teaching aid, not a security verdict, and it doesn't store or transmit what you paste.

The purpose of this project

Everything here is built to make one habit more automatic: pausing before you click. That pause is most of what phishing awareness actually is.

LearnPhish — a phishing-awareness education project. Not a real security product.

Built for learning, not for collecting your data.
