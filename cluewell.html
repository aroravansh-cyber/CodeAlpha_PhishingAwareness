ClueWell-Mail

An email analysis tool that explains the signals behind a suspicious message.

What is ClueWell-Mail?

A companion tool to LearnPhish that helps you spot suspicious signals in an email and understand why they deserve a closer look.

Find suspicious signals hidden in emails.

Enter the sender, subject, and body of an email. ClueWell-Mail highlights the signals that may need further investigation and explains each one, instead of just calling the email safe or phishing.

ClueWell-Mail opens in a new tab, so you can keep this page open while you try it.

Visit ClueWell-Mail

What is it used for?

Use it to check the parts of an email that phishing attempts usually rely on.

Sender details

Looks for unusual sender information or domains.

Message signals

Highlights urgency, pressure, or suspicious requests.

Links & domains

Points out links that may require verification.
